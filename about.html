About Our Club

San Jacinto Rotary Club

Serving the heart of the San Jacinto Valley with Service Above Self since our charter, around 1954.

Who We Are

A Legacy of Local Service

San Jacinto Rotary Club, formally organized as San Jacinto Community Rotary Club, is a nonprofit civic organization made up of local business and community leaders united by a shared commitment to "Service Above Self." Chartered around 1954, we're one of the longer-standing service clubs in the San Jacinto Valley, with more than seven decades of fellowship and community impact.

We are organized as a California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation (filed October 12, 2016, status active and in good standing), and our related 501(c)(3) entity operates under EIN 82-1237095. Our principal address is 1460 Caraway Ct, San Jacinto, CA 92582.

Our Mission

Building a Better Valley, Together

We are dedicated to identifying and addressing local needs through service projects that promote goodwill, opportunity, and understanding. Alongside Rotary International's global mission, our local expression includes honoring community leaders, supporting students, and standing behind the people who keep San Jacinto strong — including veterans, teachers, and seniors.

Consistent with Rotary International's core values, we promote integrity and advance world understanding, goodwill, and peace, one project at a time.
