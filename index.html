Who We Are

Serving the San Jacinto Valley with Pride

Since our inception, the Rotary Club of San Jacinto has brought together civic-minded leaders to exchange ideas, form lifelong friendships, and lead community service projects.

We honor community heroes, award scholarships to high school seniors, support local food drives, and participate in Rotary International's mission to eradicate polio worldwide.

Our Impact

How We Serve Our Community

Through hands-on projects, sponsorships, and partnerships, we tackle pressing challenges in San Jacinto.

Youth & Education

Providing high school scholarships, sponsoring RYLA youth leadership retreats, and honoring teachers of the year.

Community Recognition

Annual Citizen of the Year awards, honoring local firefighters, police officers, and community volunteers.

Global Humanitarian Work

Partnering with Rotary International to eradicate polio, fund clean water projects, and advance global health.

The Rotary Four-Way Test

Of the things we think, say or do:

1st

Is it the TRUTH?

2nd

Is it FAIR to all concerned?

3rd

Will it build GOODWILL & Better Friendships?

4th

Will it be BENEFICIAL to all concerned?
