Welcome to San Jacinto Rotary

Service Above Self Since 1954

Rotary International is a global network of 1.4 million neighbors, friends, leaders, and problem-solvers who see a world where people unite and take action to create lasting change across the globe, in our communities, and in ourselves.

The Rotary Club of San Jacinto brings together dedicated individuals who are passionate about improving lives in the San Jacinto Valley. Through local literacy projects, youth scholarships, food distribution, and community support, our members actively serve with purpose and camaraderie.

We invite you to join us for lunch as a guest and experience firsthand how Rotary brings good people together for great causes!

15th Annual "Peach of a Deal" Fundraiser

Our annual peach and apple sale is underway, with delivery expected in early September. Every box purchased helps fund books for San Jacinto students, college scholarships, and Leadership Camp.

The O'Henry peach is a late-summer freestone variety, sweet and juicy, excellent for salads, baking, sorbets, preserves, sauces, smoothies, and plain eating. The Honeycrisp apple brings sweetness, firmness, and tartness, perfect for eating fresh or baking into a pie.

What Your Support Funds

Every Project Strengthens Our Valley

When you purchase peaches and apples — or join us for a meeting — you help place books in the hands of San Jacinto students and support our year-round community programs.

Books for Students

Putting books directly into the hands of San Jacinto students to build a love of reading.

College Scholarships

Awarding scholarships to local high school students pursuing higher education.

Leadership Camp

Sending students to Rotary Leadership Camp to build confidence and life skills.

Veterans & Troops

Supporting veterans, active troops, and their families throughout the year.

Teachers

Recognizing and supporting the educators who shape our community's future.

Seniors

Providing assistance and fellowship opportunities for local seniors.

Community Recognition

Hosting our annual Community Recognition Dinner honoring local leaders and nonprofits.

Weekly Fellowship

Open, welcoming meetings every Wednesday for members and guests alike.

Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion
