Welcome to San Jacinto Rotary

Service Above Self Since 1922

Rotary International is a global network of 1.4 million neighbors, friends, leaders, and problem-solvers who see a world where people unite and take action to create lasting change across the globe, in our communities, and in ourselves.

The Rotary Club of San Jacinto brings together dedicated individuals who are passionate about improving lives in the San Jacinto Valley. Through local literacy projects, youth scholarships, food distribution, and community support, our members actively serve with purpose and camaraderie.

We invite you to join us for lunch as a guest and experience firsthand how Rotary brings good people together for great causes!

Guest Contact: Peaches — Club Secretary | peaches@sanjacintorotary.org
