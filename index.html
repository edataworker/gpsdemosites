Welcome to Our Club

The Rotary Club of San Jacinto meets weekly to foster fellowship and discuss meaningful ways to serve our local community and international projects.

From supporting Veterans Memorial Park initiatives to delivering food supplies and honoring outstanding community heroes, our members carry out Rotary's motto: Service Above Self.

The Rotary Four-Way Test

Of the things we think, say or do:

1st

Is it the TRUTH?

2nd

Is it FAIR to all concerned?

3rd

Will it build GOODWILL & Better Friendships?

4th

Will it be BENEFICIAL to all concerned?
