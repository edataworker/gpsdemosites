Who We Are

Service Above Self in San Jacinto

The Rotary Club of San Jacinto brings together professionals, civic leaders, and neighbors who share a passion for building a stronger, healthier community.

From partnering on Veterans Memorial Park improvements to recognizing outstanding civic leaders and distributing civics books to local schools, our members lead with action.

Hands-On Service

Making a Difference Every Day

A look at some of our recent community service initiatives in San Jacinto.

Food & Essential Relief Drives

Rotarians mobilize to unload trucks and distribute essential food and supply boxes to local families in need.

Community Recognition Awards

Annual awards night at Emilio's honoring Citizens of the Year, educators, and local first responders.

Youth Civics & Education

Sponsoring local youth programs and presenting students with U.S. Constitution and Declaration booklets.

Weekly Luncheons

Weekly Fellowship & Inspiring Speakers

Every Wednesday at Emilio's Mexican Restaurant, we host guest speakers covering local news, community programs, and charitable initiatives.

The Rotary Four-Way Test

Of the things we think, say or do:

1st

Is it the TRUTH?

2nd

Is it FAIR to all concerned?

3rd

Will it build GOODWILL & Better Friendships?

4th

Will it be BENEFICIAL to all concerned?
