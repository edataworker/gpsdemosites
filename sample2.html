Welcome to San Jacinto Rotary

Rotary International is the world's premier volunteer service organization. In San Jacinto, our members are local business leaders, professionals, educators, and retirees who gather weekly for fellowship, inspiring guest speakers, and project planning.

From supporting local high school students with scholarships to recognizing local first responders and teachers, our club provides hands-on leadership where it matters most.

Where We Focus Our Energy

Youth & Education

Empowering local students through youth leadership grants, literacy drives, and annual high school senior scholarships.

Community Service

Hands-on local initiatives, supporting food banks, and celebrating San Jacinto's Citizen of the Year.

Leadership & Recognition

Honoring local educators, police officers, firefighters, and unsung community heroes every year.

Commitment to Diversity, Equity & Inclusion

"At Rotary, we understand that cultivating a diverse, equitable, and inclusive culture is essential to realizing our vision of a world where people unite and take action to create lasting change."
