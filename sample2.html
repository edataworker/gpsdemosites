How You Can Help Change The World - While Having Fun!

Join the Rotary Club of San Jacinto. (Check Out Our Facebook Page!)

Rotary International is the world's premier volunteer service organization, with more than 1.2 million members in 33,000 clubs worldwide. Rotary Club of San Jacinto members are local business leaders, community professionals, and retirees who meet weekly to combat hunger, improve local literacy, support youth initiatives, and promote peace under the motto "Service Above Self."

We meet every Wednesday over lunch while listening to great civic speakers, connecting with community partners, and organizing service projects while having a lot of fun!

Rotary's Policy of Diversity, Equity and Inclusion

We believe that all people hold visible and invisible qualities that inherently make them unique, and we strive to create an inclusive culture where each person knows they are valued and belong.
