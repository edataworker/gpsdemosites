How You Can Help Change The World - While Having Fun!

Join the Phoenix West Rotary Club. (Check Out Our Facebook Page!)

Rotary International is the world's oldest volunteer service organization, with more than 1.2 million members in 33,000 clubs worldwide. Phoenix West Rotary Club members are local business leaders, professionals, and retirees who meet weekly and work locally, regionally, and internationally to combat hunger, improve health and sanitation, provide education and job training, and promote peace under the motto "Service Above Self."

We meet on Thursdays over lunch while listening to great speakers, talking with elected officials, meeting with business leaders, and planning service projects while having a lot of fun! If you're ready to make a difference in the world while enjoying the company of others like you, join us!
