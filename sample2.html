Welcome to San Jacinto Rotary

We are a vibrant club of business professionals, educators, and community leaders who meet weekly to plan service projects, hear inspiring speakers, and build friendships.

From scholarships for local high school students to honoring first responders and teachers, our hands-on approach makes a tangible difference. Rotary International unites 1.4 million members worldwide — and we're proud to represent San Jacinto.

Where We Focus

Our service projects reflect the heart of San Jacinto — we invest in youth, community, and leadership.

Youth & Education

Scholarships, literacy programs, and youth leadership grants that empower the next generation.

Community Service

Food drives, park cleanups, and celebrating local heroes — from teachers to first responders.

Leadership & Recognition

Honoring Citizen of the Year, police officers, firefighters, and educators who shape our community.

Diversity, Equity & Inclusion

At Rotary, we cultivate a diverse, equitable, and inclusive culture to realize our vision of a world where people unite and take action to create lasting change.
