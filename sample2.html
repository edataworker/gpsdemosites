Welcome to San Jacinto Rotary

Rotary International is the world's oldest volunteer service organization, with more than 1.2 million members in 33,000 clubs worldwide. San Jacinto Rotary Club members are local business leaders, professionals and retirees who meet weekly and work locally, regionally, and internationally to combat hunger, improve health and sanitation, provide education and job training, and promote peace under the motto "Service Above Self."

From supporting local high school students with scholarships to recognizing local first responders and teachers, our club provides hands-on leadership where it matters most. Learn more about becoming a member →

Where We Focus Our Energy

Strong fellowship among Rotarians and meaningful community and international service projects characterize our work.

Youth & Education

Empowering local students through youth leadership grants, literacy drives, and annual high school senior scholarships.

Community Service

Hands-on local initiatives, supporting food banks, and celebrating San Jacinto's Citizen of the Year.

Leadership & Recognition

Honoring local educators, police officers, firefighters, and unsung community heroes every year.

Rotary's Policy of Diversity, Equity & Inclusion
