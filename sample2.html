Welcome to the Rotary Club of San Jacinto (Follow us on Facebook)

The Rotary Club of San Jacinto unites community leaders, business professionals, and dedicated volunteers committed to making a tangible impact across the San Jacinto Valley. Guided by Rotary International’s core motto, "Service Above Self," our members spearhead local civic initiatives, support youth development, and deliver hands-on community assistance year-round.

We meet weekly over lunch to exchange ideas, hear from civic guest speakers, and coordinate initiatives ranging from local food drives and teacher recognitions to youth leadership scholarships.

The Rotary 4-Way Test

Of the things we think, say or do:

1st Is it the TRUTH?
2nd Is it FAIR to all concerned?
3rd Will it build GOODWILL & Better Friendships?
4th Will it be BENEFICIAL to all concerned?
