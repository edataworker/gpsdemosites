About the District

Inland Southern California

Rotary International District 5330 serves the San Jacinto Valley and the broader Riverside and San Bernardino Counties region, connecting 61 clubs — including San Jacinto Rotary Club — in a shared mission of service, fellowship, and international goodwill.

Through District 5330, our club taps into training resources, grant opportunities, and district-wide youth and community programs that extend our local impact far beyond San Jacinto.

Youth Leadership Spotlight

PRYDE: Personal Rotary Youth Development Experience

PRYDE is District 5330's dedicated leadership retreat for 7th-grade students, focused on self-leadership as the foundation for leading others — a natural stepping stone toward Interact and RYLA.

Program Details

  • Who7th-grade students who live or attend school in District 5330
  • FormatTwo-day overnight leadership retreat
  • VenueThousand Pines Conference Center, Crestline, CA
  • Typical CostApproximately $240–$250, often covered by the sponsoring Rotary club
  • How to ApplyThrough your local Rotary club, including San Jacinto Rotary

What Students Learn

PRYDE uses a Jump Start Leadership curriculum that introduces students to Rotary's purpose while building practical life skills, including:

  • Achieving goals
  • Managing time
  • Tapping into talent
  • Taking risks and overcoming obstacles

Participants also take part in a service project focused on youth of the world, and the program fee typically includes transportation, lodging, meals, materials, a sweatshirt, and a camp photo.
