Rotary International District 5330

San Jacinto Rotary Club is proud to serve alongside more than 60 sister clubs across Inland Southern California.

About the District

Inland Southern California

Rotary International District 5330 serves the San Jacinto Valley and the broader Riverside and San Bernardino Counties region, connecting 61 clubs — including San Jacinto Rotary Club — in a shared mission of service, fellowship, and international goodwill.

District Youth Programs

  • EarlyAct (elementary & middle school)
  • PRYDE – Personal Rotary Youth Development Experience (7th grade)
  • Interact (high school)
  • RYLA – Rotary Youth Leadership Awards
